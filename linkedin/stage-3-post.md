Last week I shared three findings from my customer support project. This week I tried to prove myself wrong.

In SQL, Samsung Galaxy, Sony 4K TV and Google Nest all looked like "problem products", with more defect tickets than the average. Critical tickets also seemed to be stuck just as much as Low ones. And one product had the worst satisfaction score.

But each product only has around 200 tickets. So before telling anyone "fix this product", I wanted to know: is this real, or just chance?

So I went to Python and tested each one. I even simulated a world where every product is exactly the same, and the numbers still spread out a lot just by luck.

The result surprised me. Only one finding survived. Samsung Galaxy is really different, even after correcting for the fact that I was comparing 42 products. Sony and Google Nest were just noise. The satisfaction difference was noise too.

And the priority one? Still true. There's no evidence that Critical tickets move faster than Low ones, and honestly, that's the most useful thing I found for the business.

Biggest lesson for me: finding a pattern is easy. Checking if it's real is the part that actually matters.

Next step is Power BI, putting all of this into a dashboard.

Have you ever had a finding fall apart when you tested it?
